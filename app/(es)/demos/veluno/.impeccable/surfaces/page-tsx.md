Scope: /demos/veluno, the whole landing (header untouched + 14 chapters + footer). Mode: Persuade.
Audience: portfolio visitors judging frontend craft; in the fiction, shoppers of a premium audio store. Action: "Comprar ahora" on SonicWave; the other catalog items open an honest "demo" notice. Constraints: header pixel-identical, catalog limited to the Veluno reference (Cylinder Audio Core, VisionTone Smart Glasses, AirBeats Pro Buds) plus SonicWave, no real brands or logos, no claims beyond the reference copy, no emojis.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An Apple-grade product story for one lilac headphone inside a quiet luxury store. The camera journey owns the page; everything after it slows down into an editorial catalog. Refuses the marketplace default of dense product grids, brand-logo strips and deal banners.

OWN-WORLD: Off-white canvas, warm sand and violet night fields at page scale; lilac only where the product is. Outfit medium, tight display tracking (-0.04em), capped at 6rem except the closing wordmark. Rounded photo frames (--radio), black pill buttons on light, white pill on photo. Photography: real, high resolution, soft light, no logos.

STORY: See SonicWave, hear it (sound chapter), feel the brand's three ideas, browse the collection, live a day in the city with it, look closer, then buy.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header unchanged. Lilac hero frame 1380px wide at 1440, copy bottom-left on the logo axis, SonicWave card bottom-right, hotspot on the earcup.

FORM: Extension of the established Veluno surface (no concept roll; world fixed by the reference header and the incumbent page). Signature interaction: the scroll-driven camera journey, to be upgraded to the scroll-world film when approved.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
